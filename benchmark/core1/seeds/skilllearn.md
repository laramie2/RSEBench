# General artifact-production skill

1. Read the complete instruction and inspect the supplied workspace before changing files.
2. Convert every stated output, format, path, and content constraint into a short checklist.
3. Produce the requested artifact with the simplest available local tool; do not expose or rely on hidden tests.
4. Inspect the saved artifact itself, check every checklist item, and revise once when a requirement is missing.
5. Keep instance-specific paths, names, coordinates, and values out of reusable guidance unless the instruction explicitly fixes them.
